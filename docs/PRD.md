# Product Requirements: OwnLedger

_Last updated: 2026-10-24_

## 1. Overview
**OwnLedger** is an installable, offline-capable web app (PWA) for tracking personal expenses and expenses shared in groups (trips, home, couples, others). Everything is stored locally on the user's device. Groups are shared between members through a Google Drive folder. There is no backend.

## 2. Users
- A person tracking their own spending.
- Small groups (friends, family, partners) with Google accounts who share and split expenses.

## 3. Platforms
- Android and iOS (installable PWA), plus desktop browsers.
- Hosted on GitHub Pages at https://omkarsheral1989.github.io/ownledger/ (ADR-032). Addresses inside the app, such as a group's page, also work when opened or reloaded directly.
- Works offline after the first load. Sign-in, backup and sync need a network.

## 4. Features

### 4.1 Authentication
- **No account needed.** The welcome page's one button is "Open OwnLedger" (5.1). It opens a page (5.1b) with **Sign in with Google**, "OR" and **Continue as a guest**, and a note that Google sign-in is required to back up data and share it with others.
- **Guest:** a guest uses the whole app on this device, with no account. Their own person is saved as "Guest" (no email) and screens say "You". Nothing is backed up or shared, and a guest cannot invite members to share a group, until they sign in with Google.
- **One set of data per device.** There is no separate guest data and signed-in data. Signing in with Google makes that account the owner of the data already on the device, so what a guest made is theirs, and nothing is copied or asked about. The user's person gets the account's email, and the account's name (and photo) only while nobody has named them: a name another member typed for them is kept (5.8, Names). If that email already belongs to a member someone added earlier, the guest person is merged into that member (their expenses and shares become theirs) so there is one person.
- **Sign out** clears the session but keeps the data on the device. The user can then "Continue as a guest" and still sees everything made while signed in.
- **One Google account per device.** Once the data belongs to an account, a different Google account signing in on this device is refused with a message naming the owner ("This device is already used by a@gmail.com. Sign in with that account, or clear the app's data to use another."). The data is never shown to or taken over by another account.
- Google sign-in asks for profile and email only. Google Drive access is requested later, the first time the user backs up or syncs.
- The signed-in user's email, name and photo are used as identity. The app remembers the user (or the guest) on the device, so it opens offline without signing in again.
- Signing in or out (or starting or leaving guest mode) in one browser tab applies to all open tabs of the app. Only one tab at a time can open the data; another tab shows a message with a "Try again" button.

### 4.2 Groups
- Create a group with a name, a type (trip / home / couple / other, each with its own icon), a default currency and members.
- Members are picked from people already known on this device (people from the user's other groups), or added through an "Add a new member" dialog (a name, and optionally a Gmail address). Typing an address into the members field no longer adds anyone. A person's email is optional; when given it must be an `@gmail.com` address, which means Google Workspace accounts with other domains cannot be added.
- The creator is always a member of the new group.
- Any member can edit the group's name, type and default currency (5.3a). Any member can add people to an existing group from its members sheet (5.4); removing members is not built yet.
- Members who have not opened the app yet are shown as pending. A member without an email is a member on this device only (a payer, in splits and balances); they cannot be invited to share the group through Drive until an email is added, which is not built yet.

### 4.3 Expenses
- Personal expenses (private, never synced to Drive) and group expenses.
- Fields: description, amount, currency, date, category (fixed default list), tags (later), notes, receipt photos, payer, split.
- Any group member can edit or delete any group expense. Latest edit wins, and the edit history is kept. Editing (5.6a), deleting and restoring (5.6b) and going back to an earlier version (5.6c) are built. Payments between members can be deleted but not edited yet.
- Group expenses are added from the group page (5.5). Personal expenses arrive later.
- Adding an expense counts as activity in its group, so the group moves to the top of the home page list.

### 4.4 Splitting
- Equal split, with either the user or another member as payer.
- Paid for self / paid for another member only.
- Owed the full amount (user or another member).
- Split by percentage, by exact amount, by shares, or by adjustment (details in 5.5).
- Pending members (not yet signed in) can be payers and be part of splits.
- Rounding: money is whole minor units, so a split that does not divide evenly leaves a few minor units over. The payer absorbs them, even when the payer is not part of the split.

### 4.5 Balances and settle-up
- Per group balances: who owes whom, per currency. Balances are pairwise (each person against each other person), one line per person per currency, for example "Priya owes you ₹14,517.50".
- On the home page a group row shows the user's net balance per currency.
- Record settlement payments between members (5.7): who paid whom, how much and when, saved as a payment that counts against what they owe each other.
- Multi-currency without conversion: each currency is tracked separately.

### 4.6 Receipt photos
- Up to 10 photos per expense, added on the add-expense page and shown on the expense details page (5.5, 5.6).
- Photos are stored locally on the device (IndexedDB, one store per account) at original size, with a thumbnail for lists.
- On Backup or Sync, photos upload to Google Drive. On Sync, missing photos from other members are downloaded.
- Sync downloads all photos before finishing, and is resumable.

### 4.7 Backup and sync
- **Where it lives:** one private **OwnLedger** folder in the user's Google Drive, holding a private **Backup** folder (never shared) and a **Groups** folder with one folder per group, each shared with that group's members. A group another member created is in the user's "Shared with me"; the app adds a shortcut to it in their own OwnLedger/Groups folder. Details in 5.8.
- **Backup:** a copy of everything on the device (every group, person and expense with its history, and later personal expenses) and **all** receipt photos, in the private Backup folder, replaced on each backup.
- **Restore:** there is no separate Restore (phase 6). Every full Sync first adds the backup to what is on the device: for each item the newer version wins, nothing on the device is wiped, and missing photos are downloaded.
- **Group sync:** pushes the user's own changes and pulls other members' changes from the group's shared folder, then merges them locally (newest change wins per item; edit history is kept).
- **Starting it:** manually only. A Sync button in the home page header backs up and syncs every group; a Sync button on a group page syncs that group only. Nothing syncs by itself.
- Sync shows progress (data, then photos) and handles failures and token expiry. Guests are asked to sign in with Google first.

### 4.8 Reports and tools
- Monthly totals by category (personal and per group).
- Search and filters (date range, category, tag, member, currency, amount).
- Budgets (monthly limits per category).
- CSV export.

### 4.9 PWA
- Installable on Android and iOS, works offline, shows a prompt when a new version is available.

## 5. Pages

**Message screens:** a screen that is only a message ("Group not found", "Expense not found", "Couldn't load …" with Try again, "OwnLedger is open in another tab", "Couldn't open your data", and "Opening your data…") is centered vertically and horizontally: on the whole screen when the page has no header, and in the space below the header when it has one (the home page's "Couldn't load your groups" is centered between its heading and the floating Create group button).

**Browser tab titles:** each signed-in page sets the tab's title: "Your groups · OwnLedger" (home), the group's name followed by " · OwnLedger" (group page), "Create a group · OwnLedger", "Add expense · OwnLedger" and the expense's description followed by " · OwnLedger" (expense details; "Expense not found · OwnLedger" for a missing one).

**Header titles:** a page that has a Back button (the shared `BackButton`: a 40 px outlined square with an arrow, the look of the Edit and Delete buttons at the other end) titles its header with a quiet heading beside it, 16 px and medium weight (`HEADER_TITLE_STYLE`), smaller than the content's own title below it: "Expense" or "Payment" on an expense's details (5.6; "Deleted expense" or "Deleted payment" once deleted), "Add expense", "Edit expense", "Version" (5.6c), "Settle up", "Create a group" and "Edit group".

7. **Footer:** "free and open source (GPL-3.0)" with a link to the GitHub repository.

Copy rules: claims must stay true. "No ads and no tracking" holds only while no analytics or tracking is added to the app.

### 5.3 Create group
Like the add-expense and expense-details pages, it has no card around its content: the form sits directly on the page background (the thin dividers inside stay).
- Route `/groups/new`, reached from the floating "Create group" button on the home page. Back and the browser's reload warning work as described under Leaving.
- **Name:** required, 1 to 60 characters.
- **Type:** trip / home / couple / other, each shown as an Ant Design icon in a colored tile. "Trip" is preselected.
- **Default currency:** required. A searchable list (by code or name) of all ISO currencies, each shown as code, name and symbol (for example "INR – Indian Rupee (₹)"), with names in the user's language. The currency of the device's region (for example INR in India) is preselected and listed first; the rest follow A to Z. New expenses in the group start with it; an expense can still use any other currency, since each expense carries its own.
- **Members:** one searchable multi-select. It lists people already known on this device (everyone saved here, with name and email, never the user themself); a person without an email shows "No email" where the email would be. Typing only searches the list (by name or email); it never adds anyone. "Add a new member" is always the last row of the list. The creator is added automatically and cannot be removed.
  - **Add a new member dialog** (a centered dialog on desktop, a bottom sheet on phones): a **Name** (required, 1 to 60 characters) and an **Email** (optional; if given, an `@gmail.com` address). Cancel and Add. Add saves the person on this device right away, so they are in the list for every other group even if this group is not created, then closes the dialog and selects them as a member of this group. Problems are shown beside their field when Add is pressed:
    - **Same email:** if the email already belongs to someone on this device, "Priya Shah is already in your list with this email." Nothing is added.
    - **Same name:** names are unique on this device (ignoring case and extra spaces): "You already have someone called Sam." This includes the user themself and people who have no email.
    - An address that is not `@gmail.com` is refused with the same message as before.
- **Validation:** checked only when "Create" is pressed. Every problem (empty name, no currency) is then shown at once, beside its field. The members field has none of its own: a person is checked when added in the dialog.
  - **Same name:** a group cannot have the same name as another group the user belongs to (ignoring case, extra spaces and deleted groups). The message names the existing group ("You already have a group called Goa trip.") and appears beside the name field. This is a convenience check on this device; another member can still create a group with the same name elsewhere, and both groups then simply exist.
  - **Duplicate members:** choosing the same person twice selects them once, and the user is never in the list, since they are added automatically.
- **Leaving:** the page's Cancel and Back buttons leave immediately while the form is untouched. Once a name, type, currency or member has been entered or changed, they ask "Discard this group?" first, and reloading or closing the tab shows the browser's own warning. The browser's Back button is not intercepted: that needs React Router's data router, which the app does not use yet.
- Creating a group only saves it on this device. Sharing it through Drive happens later, at the first sync.
- After creating, a "Group created." message appears and the app opens the new group's page.
- A message under a field disappears as soon as that field is edited.

### 5.3a Edit group
Route `/groups/<id>/edit`, opened by the pencil button on the group page's band. Any member can use it. Like Create group (5.3), the form sits directly on the page background, with Back, the heading "Edit group", and Cancel and Save.
- **Fields:** the group's **name**, **type** and **default currency**, filled in with what the group has now, with the same rules and the same pickers as Create group. There is no members field (members are not edited here yet). The currency's note says new expenses start with it and existing expenses keep theirs: nothing already saved changes.
- **Validation:** checked only when Save is pressed, every problem at once beside its field, a message disappearing as soon as its field is edited. **Same name:** a group cannot take the name of another group the user belongs to (ignoring case, extra spaces and deleted groups), and the message names that group ("You already have a group called Flat bills."). The group's own name is never "taken", so only changing its case works.
- **Saving:** shows "Group updated." and returns to the group page, which shows the new name, type color and currency. It counts as activity: the group moves to the top of the home list. When nothing was changed, Save simply goes back, with no message and no change to the group. A failure shows "Couldn't save the changes. Please try again." and keeps what was typed. A group deleted in the meantime shows "This group is no longer available." and goes to the home page.
- **Leaving:** Back and Cancel go straight back to the group while nothing has changed (spaces around the name do not count). After a change they ask "Discard your changes?" ("What you changed will be lost.", Discard / Keep editing), and reloading or closing the tab shows the browser's own warning.
- **Other states:** grey placeholders while loading, "Couldn't load this group" with Try again, and "Group not found" (the same message as the group page) for a group that does not exist, was deleted, or that the user is not in. The tab title is "Edit group · OwnLedger".

With nobody to offer the list says "Everyone you know is already in this group." (and "No one matches." when a search finds no one).

### 5.5 Add expense
Route `/groups/<id>/expenses/new`, opened from the group page's "Add expense" button. Built in five phases (listed at the end of this section). The section also covers the expense list and balance line it brings to the group page.

**Layout and behavior**
- Phone-first single page. A header with Back and a tick (Save). Back asks "Discard this expense?" once anything (including the payer, split or photos) has been entered or changed; reloading or closing the tab shows the browser's own warning (as in create-group).
- Pickers (category, currency, date, who paid, split) open as dialogs: centered on desktop, a bottom sheet on phones. One shared adaptive component provides both.
- A **group switcher** under the header shows the group-type icon, the group's name and a small arrow, full width of the form column (no "With you and" text). It opens a menu of the user's groups with the current one ticked; with only one group it is a plain label. Choosing another group moves the expense there, with the whole group (no choosing of people): the description, category, amount, date, notes and receipts stay, the currency follows the new group's default unless the user chose another one, and the split starts again for the new group's members with the user paying. The form stays on screen while the other group loads (no loading placeholder). Back still asks to discard, since the carried fields count as input. The expense is added to the group shown.
- Form order: category and description; currency and amount; the sentence "Paid by [you] and split [equally]"; the date row; the notes box; the receipts row. The receipts row is stacked, with its icon at the start, no labels and no footer, and does not repeat the group name.
- Saving returns to the group page, shows "Expense added." and the new expense is on top of the list.

**Fields**
- **Description:** required, 1 to 100 characters.
- **Amount:** greater than 0, at most 1 billion, with the currency's decimal places. Only "." is accepted as the decimal mark. Thousands separators appear while typing (lakh style for INR, western style for other currencies) through one shared money formatter. Changing the currency rounds the amount to the new currency's decimals.
- **Category:** about 45 categories in 7 groups (Entertainment, Food and drink, Home, Life, Transportation, Uncategorized, Utilities), each with a stable key, an Ant Design icon and a color of our own (no artwork copied, ADR-030). "General" (in Uncategorized) is the default.
- **Currency:** a 40 px square button showing the currency's symbol ("$", "€", "₹"; "CA$" for a dollar the language tells apart from its own), or "฿" for a currency whose short symbol only it uses, or else its 3-letter code (a shared symbol such as a bare "$" for SGD or "kr" for SEK is never used), in a font shrunk to fit (18 px for one character, 14 px for two or three, 12 px for a code). It opens a picker with a search box (code or name), the chosen currency first under "Selected" (ticked), then a "Recent" section (up to 3: the group's default, then the currencies of the user's own latest expenses on this device), then all ISO currencies A to Z. Each currency is listed once, so the chosen one is left out of Recent and the full list. While searching, only the matches are shown (the chosen one ticked among them).
- **Date:** any date; today by default; a full-width box like the other fields (white, bordered, 40 px high, calendar icon at the start) showing it as "Today, 8 Oct 2026", "Yesterday, 7 Oct 2026", or with the weekday for other days ("Mon, 5 Oct 2026"); chosen in a calendar dialog (react-day-picker) that moves between months, the old month sliding out and the new one in, by swiping sideways, scrolling the wheel or trackpad (one month per flick), the previous and next arrows, or the year and month lists, none of which chooses a day; the chosen day is lit only in its own month. People who asked their device for less motion get the month without the slide.
- **Notes:** a box of 2 to 6 rows, at most 1000 characters, with a counter near the limit.
- **Receipts:** a camera button opens the device's own file chooser (pictures only; it offers the camera on phones), several at a time. Chosen photos appear beside it as small squares in order, each with an x to take it out. Up to 10: choosing more keeps the first ones and says "An expense can have up to 10 photos."; with 10 the button is switched off ("Up to 10 photos."). Files that are not pictures are ignored. Photos are kept on the device at original size with a thumbnail when the expense is saved, and taken off again if it is not.

**Splitting**
- Default: you paid, split equally among all members.
- **3 or more members:** the sentence has two buttons, "Paid by [you]" and "split [equally]".
- **2 members:** one button showing the current quick choice; it opens the four choices "You paid, split equally", "You are owed the full amount", "Priya paid, split equally" and "Priya is owed the full amount", plus "More options" (the split dialog). Once the split is not one of the four, the form shows the two-button sentence instead. Any non-equal split reads "unequally"; an equal split between only some people still reads "equally".
- People are named by first name; without a name, by the part of the email before the "@", with a capital first letter ("Sam" for sam@gmail.com).
- "Multiple people" as payer is shown switched off.
- A group with one member can still get expenses; the split buttons are switched off, with the tip "You are the only member of this group."
- **Who paid** (the "[you]" button): a dialog "Who paid?" listing the members ("You" first) and "Multiple people" switched off.
- **Split dialog** ("Split options"): a "Paid by" dropdown on top, then tabs Equally, Amounts, Percent, Shares, Adjust (each with a heading, a one-line explanation and an icon), one line per member with their avatar and name, and what their part comes to under the name once the split works:
  - **Equally:** tick who shares; the payer may be unticked; at least one person must be ticked.
  - **Exact amounts:** must total the expense amount.
  - **Percentages:** whole numbers, total 100.
  - **Shares:** whole numbers from 0 to 1000, at least one share in total.
  - **Adjustment:** each "+" amount is 0 or more, the adjustments add up to at most the amount, and the remainder is split equally.
- Footers show "X of TOTAL" and "N left" (green at 0) or "N over" (red). No illustrations: a heading, a one-line explanation and an Ant Design icon.
- If the split is not balanced, pressing the dialog's tick shows why ("The split no longer adds up.", "Choose at least one person.", "Give at least one share.", or "Enter the amount first." for amounts and adjustments before an amount is typed) and the dialog stays open. Editing clears the message.
- If the amount changes after exact amounts were entered, the form shows "The split no longer adds up" and Save is blocked until it is fixed.
- The method and the user's inputs are stored, so the split can be shown and edited later.

**Expense list on the group page (replaces the empty list once expenses exist)**
- Flat list, newest date first (the most recently added first within a day), with a quiet grey "September 2025" header (month in full and year) above each month's expenses, including the newest month; the headers scroll with the list. Each row: date (month over day), a colored category tile, the title with "You paid …" (or "Priya paid …") under it, and on the right "you lent" (green), "you borrowed" (orange-red), "no balance" (the user paid exactly their own share, for example in a group of one) or "not involved", with the amount.
- A **deleted** expense stays in the list as one line in its own place (the date it had, and its place among that day's expenses: the order of adding, so neither an edit nor the deletion moves it): the same date column, then a grey bin icon with no tile, and the text "You deleted 'Beach shack dinner' (£9.00)" ("Priya deleted …": first names, as in "Priya paid you"), or "You deleted a payment (You paid Priya £200.00)" for a payment. It has no "paid" line and nothing at the end of the row, and does not count towards the balances. Tapping it opens the deleted expense, read-only (5.6).
- A **payment** (5.7) is one line instead: the same date column, then its icon (two arrows, in green) with no colored tile behind it, and the text "You paid Priya £200.00", "Priya paid you £45.50" or "Priya paid Sam £10.00" (first names; the amount in the payment's own currency). It has no "paid" line under it and nothing at the end of the row, since the sentence says everything.
- The date column is 48 px high (the month 16 px and the day 32 px of line), whatever the row's content.
- Tapping a row opens the expense's details page (5.6).

**Balance line (group page)**
- One line per person per currency: "Priya owes you …" with the amount in bold green, or "You owe Priya …" with the amount in bold orange-red; "You're all settled up" when there is nothing owed. Debts between two other members are not shown.

**Phases** (each with tests and break-checks, a browser check, and a UX review by the user). All five are built (October 2026, PR #1); the user's UX review of phases 2 to 5 is still to come.
1. A simple expense (equal split, you paid) saved and listed.
2. Balances: the balance line and the home page rows.
3. Who paid, and the other split methods.
4. The expense details page (5.6).
5. Receipt photos, kept on this device (upload and download come with sync).

### 5.6 Expense details
Route `/groups/<id>/expenses/<expenseId>`, opened by tapping an expense on the group page. It shows the expense and its edit history, and leads to editing and deleting it.

- **A deleted expense** opens here too, read-only: the title "Deleted expense" ("Deleted payment" for a payment) beside Back (the header titles above, as "Expense" or "Payment" on a live one), and, instead of Edit and Delete, a **Restore** button (5.6b), a grey line "Deleted by you, 21 Oct 2026, 3:45 pm" ("Deleted by Priya Shah, …") after "Edited by", everything else as for any expense (split, notes, receipts), and a History section that is always shown and starts with "You deleted this expense · 21 Oct 2026, 3:45 pm" (see below). Its edit page shows "Expense not found" (5.6a).
- Back (to the group), an **Edit** button that opens the edit page (5.6a; switched off with "Coming soon" on a payment) and a **Delete** button that asks first (5.6b).
- The category tile, the description, the amount in large type, the category's name and the day ("Dining out · Mon, 5 Oct 2026"), "Added by you" (or the person's name) and, under it, who paid in the same grey text: "You paid £9.00" or "Priya Shah paid £9.00" (one line per payer; a payment has none, since its title says who paid whom). Once an expense has been edited, a further grey line says who edited it last and when: "Edited by you, 20 Oct 2026, 3:45 pm" ("Edited by Priya Shah, …"; the time in the device's language and clock style).
- How it was split ("Split equally", "Split by exact amounts", "Split by percentages", "Split by shares", "Split by adjustment") and one line per person who owes something, the user first: "You owe £3.00", "Priya Shah owes £6.00". A person who owes nothing, such as a payer who is owed the full amount, has no line there.
- The notes, keeping their line breaks, under "Notes" (left out when there are none).
- The receipt photos under "Receipts" as thumbnails in order; tapping one shows it at full size, with the others a swipe away. A photo that is not on this device (for example added by another member and not yet synced) shows a grey square "Not on this device". Left out when there are none.
- **History**, last on the page, after a divider and under the heading "History": shown only once the expense has been edited, deleted or restored, newest first. Each edit is a block: a header "You edited this expense · 20 Oct 2026, 6:15 pm" ("Priya Shah edited this expense · …"; a person with no name by their email; the time in the device's language and clock style), then one line per thing that edit changed, in this order:
  - "Description changed from 'Dinner' to 'Lunch'" and "Notes changed from 'x' to 'y'" ("Notes added: 'y'" when there were none, "Notes removed (were 'x')" when cleared). Text longer than 80 characters is cut with "…".
  - "Category changed from Dining out to Groceries" (the category names).
  - "Cost changed from ₹100.00 to ₹120.00"; when the currency changed too, each amount is in its own currency ("from ₹100.00 to £120.00") and there is no separate currency line; when only the currency changed, "Currency changed from INR to GBP".
  - "Date changed from 5 Oct 2026 to 6 Oct 2026".
  - "Paid by changed from you to Priya Shah".
  - "Split changed from equally to by percentages" (the method names as at the top of the split section, "Split equally" read as "equally", and "Split by exact amounts" as "by exact amounts"), or, when only the numbers changed, "Split changed" followed by one nested line per person whose entered value changed, in the units of the method: "Priya Shah: 30% to 40%", "Sam: 2 shares to 1 share", "Dev Patel: ₹100.00 to ₹150.00", "Sam: in the split to not in the split", "Omkar: +₹5.00 to +₹10.00" for an adjustment. When the method itself changed, each person's line goes from the old units to the new ("Priya Shah: in the split to 40%"), for everyone whose entered value differs once both are written out. People are named as elsewhere on the page ("you" for the user).
  - "1 photo added", "2 photos removed" (a line each when both happened).
  Every entry can be tapped and opens the expense as it was right after it (5.6c). Deleting and restoring are entries too, in time order with the edits: "You deleted this expense · 21 Oct 2026, 3:45 pm", "You restored this expense · 22 Oct 2026, 9:10 am" ("payment" for a payment), with no lines under them. Going back to an earlier version (5.6c) is an entry as well: "You restored the version from 20 Oct 2026, 6:04 pm · 21 Oct 2026, 2:10 pm", with a line for each thing that changed back, written as for an edit. They stay in the history for good, so an expense that was deleted and restored shows both, and one that was deleted several times shows each deletion.
  The last entry, the oldest, is "You added this expense · 20 Oct 2026, 5:40 pm" (who created it and when). A payment between members has no history, since it cannot be edited.
- An expense that does not exist, is in another group, or is in a group the user is not in shows "Expense not found" ("It may have been deleted, or you may not be a member of its group.") with a "Back to the group" button. Loading shows grey placeholders; a failed load shows "Couldn't load this expense" with "Try again".

### 5.6a Edit expense
Route `/groups/<id>/expenses/<expenseId>/edit`, opened by the **Edit** button on the details page (5.6). Any member of the group can edit any expense of it (4.3). It is the add-expense page (5.5) with the expense's values filled in, so every field can be changed: description, category, amount, currency, date, notes, receipts, who paid and the split (all methods). A payment between members cannot be edited yet: its Edit button stays switched off.
- **Layout:** the same form and pickers as add expense, with the header "Edit expense", Back and a tick (Save). The group stays the expense's own: the group switcher is a plain label (an expense is not moved to another group). Photos already on the expense are shown first, each with an x; new ones are added after them, up to 10 in all. The split opens as it was entered (method and each person's input).
- **Validation:** the same rules and messages as add expense, checked when Save is pressed. If the amount changes after exact amounts were entered, "The split no longer adds up" blocks Save until it is fixed.
- **Saving:** shows "Expense updated." and returns to the details page, which shows the new values and the "Edited by" line. The expense counts as activity: its group moves to the top of the home list, and the expense keeps its date's place in the list. When nothing was changed (spaces around the description and notes do not count), Save simply goes back, with no message and nothing stored. A failure shows "Couldn't save the changes. Please try again." and keeps what was typed. An expense deleted in the meantime shows "This expense is no longer available." and goes to the group page.
- **Leaving:** Back asks "Discard your changes?" ("What you changed will be lost.", Discard / Keep editing) once something has changed, and goes straight back otherwise; reloading or closing the tab shows the browser's own warning.
- **History:** every save that changes something records one event of the expense: who edited it, when (date and time), the expense exactly as it is after the edit (every field, the split and the list of photos) and which fields changed with their old and new values (description, category, amount, currency, date, notes, who paid, the split, receipts added or removed). Nothing is ever overwritten, and the version before an edit is the previous event's, so each earlier version can be shown or restored later. Adding, deleting and restoring an expense are recorded the same way (5.6b), so that everything that ever happened to an expense is kept for the Activity page (6). The details page shows it (5.6). A photo taken off an expense stays on the device, so an earlier version can still show it.
- **Other states:** grey placeholders while loading, "Couldn't load this expense" with Try again, and "Expense not found" (the same message as the details page) for an expense that does not exist, was deleted, is in another group or in a group the user is not in. The tab title is "Edit expense · OwnLedger".

### 5.6b Delete and restore expense
The **Delete** button (a bin icon) on the details page (5.6), for an expense and for a payment between members alike. Any member of the group can delete any expense of it (4.3). There is no page of its own.
- **Asking:** the button opens a dialog titled "Delete this expense?" ("Delete this payment?" for a payment) with "It will be removed from the group, and the balances will change." and the buttons Cancel and Delete (red). There is no undo.
- **Deleting:** while it works the Delete button shows it is busy and the dialog stays open. Then the dialog closes, "Expense deleted." ("Payment deleted.") is shown and the group page opens: the expense is now a "You deleted …" line in the list, the balance line and the home page's balance for the group are worked out without it, and the group moves to the top of the home list (deleting is activity, like any change). A failure shows "Couldn't delete the expense. Please try again." ("Couldn't delete the payment. …"), closes the dialog and stays on the details page. An expense that was already deleted meanwhile (for example by another member) shows "This expense is no longer available." ("This payment …" for a payment) and goes to the group page.
- **What is kept:** nothing is erased. The expense is marked deleted (when, and by whom) and leaves the balances and the edit page; its split, edit history and photos stay on the device, so it can be brought back (see Restore below). It stays in the group's list as a "You deleted …" line (5.5) and opens read-only (5.6).
- The tab and Back keep working as usual; Cancel and a click outside the dialog change nothing.
- **Restore:** a deleted expense (or payment) has a **Restore** button (an undo icon) at the right of its header, where Edit and Delete are on a live one. Any member can press it. It asks nothing: the expense is brought back at once, "Expense restored." ("Payment restored.") is shown and the user stays on the page, which now shows the expense as it was (with Edit and Delete again, no "Deleted by" line, no "Deleted expense" title) and "You restored this expense" at the top of its History. While it works the button shows it is busy. In the group, the line becomes the expense's normal row again, in its own place, the balance line and the home page's balance count it again, and the group moves to the top of the home list (restoring is activity). Its split, edit history and photos are exactly as they were. A failure shows "Couldn't restore the expense. Please try again." ("Couldn't restore the payment. …") and stays on the page. An expense that was already restored meanwhile (for example by another member) shows "This expense was already restored." ("This payment …") and the page shows it as it is now.

### 5.6c Version of an expense
Route `/groups/<id>/expenses/<expenseId>/versions/<eventId>`, opened by tapping an entry of the History on the details page (5.6): **any** entry (added, edited, deleted, restored or "restored the version"), since each one holds the expense exactly as it was right after it. It is read-only.
- **Layout:** like the details page, with Back (to the expense) and the title "Version", then a line saying what the entry is ("You edited this expense · 20 Oct 2026, 6:04 pm", the same words as in the History) and, for an edit, the bullets of what it changed, written as in the History. Under it the expense as it was: the category tile, the description, the amount in large type, the category's name and the day, who paid ("You paid £9.00"), how it was split and what each person owed then ("You owe £3.00"), the notes and the receipt photos (a photo not on this device shows the grey "Not on this device" square). It has no "Added by", "Edited by" or History: those belong to the expense, not to a version.
- **Restore this version:** a button (an undo icon) at the right of the header puts the expense back to this version: description, category, amount, currency, date, notes, who paid and the split exactly as they were, and the photos it had (a photo removed later is taken back, one added since is taken off; the pictures never left the device). It asks nothing, since the version it replaces is itself kept in the History and can be restored the same way. "Expense restored to the version from 20 Oct 2026, 6:04 pm." is shown and the details page opens, with a new first entry "You restored the version from …". It counts as activity in the group (it moves to the top of the home list) and changes the balances if the amount or the split changed. A failure shows "Couldn't restore the version. Please try again." and stays on the page.
- **When the button is missing:** on the current version (the newest entry, or any version equal to what the expense is now), where a line says "This is the current version."; on an expense that is deleted now, where a line says "Restore the expense first to restore a version." (the version still opens); and on a payment between members, which cannot be edited.
- **Other states:** grey placeholders while loading, "Couldn't load this version" with Try again, and "Version not found" ("It may have been removed, or you may not be a member of its group.", with a "Back to the expense" button) for a version that does not exist, belongs to another expense or to a group the user is not in. If, by the time the button is pressed, the expense was already put back to this version (by another member), "This version is already the current one." is shown; if it was deleted meanwhile, "Restore the expense first to restore a version."; in both the page stays and reads the expense again, so the button goes. If the user can no longer see the expense (they left the group), "This version is no longer available." is shown and the group page opens. The tab title is "Version of <description> · OwnLedger".

### 5.7 Settle up
Route `/groups/<id>/settle`, opened by the "Settle up" pill on the group page (5.4). It records that one member paid another outside the app, so it counts against what they owe each other. It looks like add expense: no card, a header with Back, the heading "Settle up" and a tick (Save), then the group's icon and name.
- **Who paid?** A dropdown of the group's members, "You" first, with you chosen. Any member can be the payer, so a payment between two other members can be recorded too.
- **Who received it?** A dropdown of the other members (the payer is left out), empty until chosen ("Choose a person"). Choosing someone as payer who is the chosen recipient empties the recipient.
- **The sentence:** once both are chosen, "You paid Priya", "Priya paid you" or "Priya paid Sam" in large type, centered, under the two dropdowns.
- **Amount:** below the sentence, the currency button (the group's default to start, the same picker as add expense) and the amount (greater than 0, at most 1 billion, with the currency's decimals).
- **Besides:** the date (today by default), notes (up to 1000 characters) and receipt photos (up to 10), the same fields as add expense.
- **Validation:** checked when Save is pressed, every problem at once beside its field ("Choose who received it.", "Enter an amount.", and the add-expense messages for the currency, date and notes). A payer cannot be their own recipient.
- **Saving:** "Payment recorded." and back to the group page, where the balance has changed and the payment is on top of the list (5.5). Receipt photos are kept first and taken off again if the payment is not saved, as for an expense. A failure shows "Couldn't record the payment. Please try again." and keeps what was typed.
- **Leaving:** Back asks "Discard this payment?" once anything has been entered or changed (a different payer counts), and reloading or closing the tab shows the browser's own warning.
- **Other states:** grey placeholders while loading, "Couldn't load this group" with Try again, and "Group not found" for a group that does not exist, was deleted or that the user is not in. The tab title is "Settle up · OwnLedger".
- **A payment's details page** (5.6) is titled "You paid Priya" (also the tab's title), shows the amount, "Settlement · Mon, 5 Oct 2026", who added it, and its notes and receipts, with no split section.

### 5.8 Sync and backup
Built in phases (listed at the end), each with tests, a browser check and a UX review by the user. All six phases are built (October 2026); the real-Drive check waits for the Google OAuth client (open questions). Exact copy, icons and the dialog's look are settled with each phase.
- **Drive layout** in the user's My Drive:
  - `OwnLedger/` (private), created on the first Sync.
  - `OwnLedger/Backup/` (private, never shared): the backup of everything on the device and `photos/` with every receipt photo.
  - `OwnLedger/Groups/<group name>/`: one folder per group the user created, shared with the group's members, holding one file per member, named by their email (`priya@gmail.com.json`; each written only by that member, and the same file after their device's data was cleared, decided) and `photos/`. Groups created by others are shortcuts here to their folders. The app finds its folders by a tag it puts on them, not by name, so renaming a group or a folder breaks nothing.
- **Buttons:** a round Sync button (a cloud with arrows, spinning while a sync runs) in the home page header beside the avatar gets the backup already in Drive and adds it to the device, syncs every group, and then writes the backup (phase 6); resting the pointer on it says when the last sync finished ("Last synced 5 min ago"). A round Sync button on the group page's band syncs that group only. Both are started only by a press: there is no automatic sync.
- **Drive access:** the first Sync asks Google for access to Drive (a second consent screen after sign-in, 4.1), for the signed-in account. Access lasts about an hour; after that the next Sync asks again. If the user closes Google's window or does not allow Drive: "OwnLedger needs access to your Drive to sync. Press Sync to try again."; if they pick another Google account there: "Choose the Google account you signed in with to sync." (nothing is backed up); if the browser blocks the window: "The sign-in window was blocked. Allow pop-ups and try again."
- **Guest prompt:** "Sign in to sync" with "Backup and sync use your Google Drive, so they need a Google sign-in. Your data on this device stays as it is." and Cancel and Sign in with Google.
- **Guests:** the buttons are shown; pressing one explains that sync and backup need a Google sign-in and offers Sign in with Google (the prompt above).
- **Offline:** pressing Sync without a connection says "Sync needs an internet connection."
- **Progress dialog** (a centered dialog on desktop, a bottom sheet on phones), titled "Syncing", with a Close button: one line for Backup and one per group, each going through data and then photos ("12 of 40 photos"), with a spinner while it works, a green tick when done and a red cross with the error in red when it fails (the other lines carry on). The Backup line is listed first and finishes last: "Getting your backup…", "Waiting for your groups…" while the groups sync, "Saving your data…", then "Backed up" or "Couldn't back up. Press Sync to try again." (also when the backup in Drive could not be added to the device, which then leaves it as it is). When a group stops the run before the backup is written, it reads "Not backed up: the sync stopped." It can be closed while sync goes on; pressing Sync again then opens it. It lists groups found for the first time ("New group: Goa trip"). The header's Sync icon spins while a sync runs. When it finishes, the home page reads its groups again.
- **Sharing a group:** on the first sync of a group, its creator's app creates the group's folder and shares it with every member who has an email, as an editor, and Google Drive emails them its usual "shared a folder with you" message. Members added later are shared the folder by whichever member syncs next. Until the creator has synced, another member's Sync of that group says "Waiting for Omkar to sync this group first."
- **Groups with nobody to share with** (only the user, or only members without an email) still get their folder and the user's file on Sync, ready for when someone is added (decided).
- **Folder name:** the group's folder has the group's name; when the group is renamed, the creator's next sync renames the folder too, and the other members' shortcuts follow.
- **Group lines:** a full Sync lists Backup first, then the groups in the home page's order (most recently active first), then groups found for the first time ("New group: Goa trip"). A group's line goes "Getting changes…", "Sending your changes…", then "Synced", or "Shared with Priya and Sam" when the folder was just shared with someone. A group whose creator has not synced it yet reads "Waiting for Omkar to sync this group first." with a clock instead of a tick. A failure reads "Couldn't sync this group. Press Sync to try again." followed by Drive's own reason (for example "403: The user does not have sufficient permissions for this file."); the other groups carry on. A member's file that is not valid OwnLedger data is skipped and the line ends with "Priya's changes couldn't be read." ("A member's…" when the device does not know who wrote it). If looking for groups shared with the user fails, a line "Groups shared with you" says "Couldn't look for groups shared with you. Press Sync to try again." with Drive's reason. When Drive access runs out, the line where it happened says so and no further group is synced. A group's line keeps the name the group had when its sync started.
- **Group photos (phase 4, decided):** every receipt photo of the group's expenses, including photos taken off an expense and photos of deleted expenses (as in the backup), goes to `Groups/<group name>/photos/`, one file per photo named by its id. The creator's sync makes that folder (so two members never make two); until it exists, the others' syncs move no photos. Each sync, after the data, uploads the group's photos Drive does not have yet (by whichever member has them) and downloads the ones the device does not have, with a new thumbnail; there is no warning before a large download. The group's line goes on with "Sending photos… 2 of 5", then "Getting photos… 3 of 10", then its outcome. A photo that fails is counted and the others carry on: the line ends in red with "2 photos couldn't be synced. Press Sync to try again." ("1 photo…"). A full device stops the downloads: "This device is out of space. Free some space, then sync again." A photo not synced yet shows the grey "Not on this device" square. When a line has several problems (an unreadable member's file and photos that failed), it shows each sentence, one after the other.
- **The group page's Sync button** (round, on the band) syncs only that group, without the backup, and shows the same dialog with that group's line. Resting the pointer on it says "Sync this group" before the group's first sync and "Last synced 5 min ago" after. A guest gets the sign-in prompt. When it finishes the page reads the group again.
- **Getting a shared group:** Drive cannot tell the app that something was shared, so a member gets a new group on their next full Sync (signed in with the email the group has for them): the app looks for OwnLedger group folders shared with them, and for the user's own group folders (groups they created) that the device does not have, for example after the app's data was cleared (phase 6, decided), downloads every member's file, adds the group, its members and expenses, and adds the shortcut. Their own person is joined to the one the creator added for their email.
- **Members without an email:** they are synced as people (name, shares, balances), so every member sees them; the folder is not shared with them.
- **Merging:** for each item (group, person, membership, expense, share, photo), the version changed last wins, with the person's id as tie-breaker; every event of an expense (5.6) is kept from every member. Two people with the same email are one person.
- **Names (decided):** a name someone typed for a person (in "Add a new member") is never replaced by that person's Google name. When Priya is added as "Sam" and signs in as "Samuel", every device, hers included, calls her "Sam" once they have synced, and her own row in the members sheet reads "Sam" too. A Google name is used only for a person nobody has named, for example the creator of a group, and it follows a later change of the Google name. Names that were replaced before this rule stay as they are, since a person cannot be renamed yet (open questions). Two people may end up with the same name through sync (for example one person's two accounts); both are kept, and the email under each name tells them apart (decided).
- **Backup kept:** only the latest; each backup replaces the one before.
- **Nothing in the backup is lost (phase 6, decided):** a full Sync first gets the backup already in Drive and adds it to the device (newer version of each item wins, nothing on the device is removed), and downloads the backup's photos the device does not have, with no warning. Only then do the groups sync, and the backup is written last, so it holds what the group syncs brought in too. So a device whose data was cleared, or a second device of the same account, never replaces a fuller backup with less. If the backup in Drive is not valid OwnLedger data, it is replaced without a message (decided; Drive keeps its earlier versions for about 30 days). The Backup line stays first in the dialog but finishes last.
- **Photos in the backup:** every receipt photo on the device, including photos taken off an expense and photos of deleted expenses (the app keeps them so earlier versions and restored expenses still show them). Originals only: downloading one makes its thumbnail again. Each photo is one file in `Backup/photos/`, named by its id ("3f2a…c91.jpg"); a backup uploads only the photos Drive does not have yet, and never removes one. A photo that is not on this device is skipped.
- **Photo progress:** on a full Sync the Backup line goes "Getting your backup…" (with "Downloading photos… 3 of 10" when the backup has photos the device lacks), "Waiting for your groups…" while they sync, and then "Saving your data…", then "Backing up photos… 12 of 40", then "Backed up". There is no warning before a large download.
- **Photo counts (decided):** on every photo line (Sending, Getting, Downloading and Backing up photos) the first number is the photo being worked on, not the photos finished: "Sending photos… 1 of 1" while the only photo goes, "2 of 5" for the second (the photos finished plus one, never more than the total), so a line never reads "0 of 1". A direction with nothing to move shows no photo text at all (never "0 of 0"): the line goes on to its next step or its outcome.
- **A photo that fails:** the others carry on, and the line ends in red with "2 photos couldn't be backed up. Press Sync to try again." ("1 photo couldn't…"), or, for the backup's photos the device lacks, "2 photos couldn't be downloaded. Press Sync to try again." (phase 6, decided). The data is merged and saved either way. "Last synced" is when the data was last backed up; photos that failed show on the line, and the next Sync tries them again. If the device runs out of space while downloading the backup's photos, downloading stops and the line says "This device is out of space. Free some space, then sync again."; the data stays merged.
- **Restore from Drive** (removed in phase 6, decided: it was confusing next to Sync, which now does the same and more; what it did is kept here for history): ran at once without asking, since it never removes anything, and shows its progress. It downloads the backup, adds it to what is on the device (the newer version of each item wins, nothing is wiped), then downloads the photos the device does not have (phase 2), and shows "Backup restored.". Without a backup the line says "No backup found in your Drive."; a backup that is not valid OwnLedger data changes nothing and says "The backup in your Drive couldn't be read."; any other failure, "Couldn't restore the backup. Please try again." If the backup knows the user under another id (for example from another device), the two are one person afterwards (see Merging).
- **Failures:** each step can be repeated safely, so pressing Sync again carries on where it stopped. A file in Drive that is not valid OwnLedger data is skipped and reported, never imported. Three failures stop the whole run, with one message on the line where they happened, and nothing more starts (decided):
  - **Drive access ran out:** "Sign-in to Drive expired. Press Continue to carry on." The dialog's footer then has a **Continue** button left of Close: it asks Google again (Google needs the click) and runs the same sync again, which carries on where it stopped.
  - **The connection dropped:** "Lost the connection. Press Sync to try again."
  - **The user's Google Drive is full:** "Your Google Drive is full. Free some space in Drive, then sync again."
- **Retries (decided):** a request Drive refuses for a moment (too many requests, or a server error) is tried again by itself up to 3 times, after about 1, 2 and 4 seconds; the user sees nothing unless every try fails.
- **Leaving while it runs (decided):** closing or reloading the tab during a sync shows the browser's own "Leave site?" warning.
- **A deleted group folder (decided):** if a group's folder is gone from Drive (deleted or in the bin), the creator's next sync makes it again and shares it again with every member (Drive emails them again); the data comes back from each device's copy. Members' shortcuts point at the new folder once they sync.
- **Last synced in the dialog (decided):** once a run has ended, the footer says when the last sync finished, at its left ("Last synced 5 min ago"): of everything for a full Sync, of the group for the band's button, and nothing before the first sync.
- **Phases:**
  1. Drive access, the OwnLedger folders, backup of the data, Restore from Drive (data), the header Sync button, the progress dialog with its Backup line, and the guest prompt. Built.
  2. Backup and restore of photos (decisions above). Built.
  3. Group sync of data: creating and sharing the folder, each member's file, merging, getting shared groups, shortcuts, the band button. Built.
  4. Group photos (decisions above; no warning before a large download, decided). Built.
  5. Failures: Drive access running out (Continue), a dropped connection, a full Drive, retries, the leave warning, a deleted group folder, "Last synced" in the dialog (decisions above). Built.
  6. A cleared device, and no Restore: Restore from Drive leaves the avatar menu, with its code and copy; a full Sync adds the backup in Drive to the device (data and photos) before writing it, writes it after the groups, and finds the user's own group folders as well as those shared with them (decisions above). Built.
